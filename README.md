# OpenEE Lab

**Status:** Planning — Phase 0, Stage 1 product documentation. No platform features are implemented in this repository.

OpenEE Lab is an open-source, extensible, browser-based electrical engineering platform in planning. Its intended scope spans engineering work, simulation, extension, community exchange, and learning. Communication systems are the first proposed working domain, not the platform's identity.

## Long-term product pillars

- **Engineering Workspace:** specialized editors and tools for constructing and analyzing systems.
- **Built-in Simulations:** ready-to-use experiments across electrical engineering disciplines.
- **Plugin Ecosystem:** extensible components, engines, tools, and integrations.
- **Community:** publish, discover, share, and fork projects and extensions.
- **Learning:** guided experiments, explanations, and visualizations.

These are long-term aims, not implemented features. The platform foundation calls for UI-independent simulation, domain-appropriate models, and portable projects. The existing university AM assignment, a conventional AM architecture-validation slice, and the [proposed first public release](docs/product/scope.md) have distinct scopes; none defines the long-term ecosystem.

## Development and contributions

The project is in product planning. The university AM simulator already covers conventional AM, DSB-SC, SSB, and VSB, but its numerical correctness and architecture have not been audited. Assignment integration may include those modes after audit; the proposed public release does not automatically inherit them. Technical architecture, design, implementation, validation, and release criteria remain to be decided.

Feedback on scope, learning outcomes, acceptance criteria, numerical validation, accessibility, and self-hosting expectations is welcome. Proposed decisions in the documentation require review before they become commitments. No contribution workflow is established yet.
