## Suggested sequencing and parallel work

T-01–T-03 establish decisions and guardrails. T-04 can proceed alongside T-05 but reuse decisions wait for its audit. T-06–T-08 form the UI-independent core; T-09 can begin in parallel once the catalog model is known. T-12–T-17 form the **hard M3 checkpoint**. T-15 proves a portable round trip with a provisional versioned file before T-17, while T-18 production schema approval and T-19 hardened import/export wait until after the checkpoint. T-21–T-24 can run in parallel after integrated workflows exist; T-25 collects their evidence.

## Trace and change control

The [MVP matrix](mvp.md) maps capabilities to Stage 1 IDs. The [validation plan](validation-plan.md) maps evidence to each decision; [milestones](milestones.md) define release-blocking exits. If T-17 fails, do not close FR-005, FR-006, or FR-014; request explicit product-scope review. A passing T-15 spike does not approve D-03 or substitute for T-18–T-19. New domains, hosted services, and plugins require separate future plans.
